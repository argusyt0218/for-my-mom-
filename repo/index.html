From the little kid I was to the person I’m still becoming.

Mom, I want you to know that I see everything you have done for me.

You gave me the gift of an education from my very first year of school. You kept choosing my future, kept believing in me, and kept making sacrifices so I could have opportunities. Even now, as I transferred schools in my senior year, you found a way to support me again. I know how much my tuition means, and I will never take it for granted.

We have shared so much of life with Grandma, and I’m grateful for the love and care that surrounded us. But through every season, you have been my mom, my guide, and the person who kept showing up for me. Being your only child makes the bond we share feel even more precious.

I also know I haven’t always made it easy. I argued with you, got stubborn, and let my temper speak before my heart did. I’m sorry for the times I hurt you or made you feel unappreciated. You deserved more patience from me. I’m growing up, and I want to keep becoming a son you can feel proud of.

I’m graduating in 2027. When I picture that day, I won’t only think about a diploma. I’ll think about all the years you carried me toward it. A part of every achievement I reach will always belong to you.

Thank you for loving me through every version of myself. I love you more than I know how to put into words, but I’m going to keep trying.

Dear Mom,

I may not say it enough, but I notice your love in the things you do: in the hard decisions, in the care you give, and in how you keep making room for me to grow.

Thank you for giving me a childhood, an education, and a place in this world that feels like home. Thank you for being patient while I learn how to be better. I’m sorry for the arguments and for the moments I made you feel like I didn’t care. I care more than I have ever been able to say.

When I graduate in 2027, please know that you’ll be standing there in every step I take across that stage. I hope I can give back even a little of the happiness and pride you have given me.

Whatever comes next, you will always be my mom, my first home, and one of the greatest blessings in my life.

I love you, always.
Your son
